# Waydean video backgrounds

These are locally generated, silent H.264 loops prepared for compositing behind the family tree. Artwork was generated as original still imagery, then animated and encoded in Chrome with WebCodecs and Mediabunny. No stock footage or external video service was used.

## Video format

- 1280 × 720, landscape 16:9
- H.264 / AVC in MP4, 24 constant frames per second
- 40 seconds per loop (960 frames)
- No audio track
- Approximately 1.2 Mbps; about 5.8–6.0 MiB per clip
- Posters: 640 × 360 JPEG

## Motion and loop

The mountain and village scenes use a restrained, cyclic camera push and slight horizontal/vertical drift. The camera returns to its starting framing at the loop boundary. The eagle scene uses a separate transparent eagle silhouette, tinted muted gold, that glides across the upper part of the frame and exits before the cycle restarts. Each scene has a darkened center and soft edge vignette to keep the family tree readable.

## Limits

These are animated still backgrounds, not footage generated by a text-to-video model. The mountains, towers, and village do not have independently simulated weather, water, or moving foliage. The eagle is a separate silhouette with a simple glide, not detailed wing-flapping animation. The clips were validated locally for dimensions, codec, duration, frame count/rate, and absence of audio; device playback and final tree compositing still need to be checked in the app.
